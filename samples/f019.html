3.2 Filtering

3.2-6. Turning filtering off altogether

On a read-only screen you may not want filtering at all — showFilter:false removes it. Switching it off also clears any conditions already applied, so every row comes back.

  • Filter Role so that fewer rows are shown.
  • Press Filtering on / off — the funnels vanish and all the rows return.
  • Turn it back on and the funnels return, but with no conditions set.
