3.4 Grouping and tree

3.4-1. Grouping rows with totals

Rows are gathered by the value of a column and each group gets a header carrying count, average and sum. It gives you a per-department headcount and salary total without building a separate screen.

  • Press Group rows — rows are gathered by role.
  • Read Count, Avg and Sum on each group header.
  • Click a group header to collapse that group.
