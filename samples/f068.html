8. Change tracking

8-10. Turning the change colours off (dirtyMark)

New rows are shaded green, edited ones amber and deleted ones red. If that is too much, dirtyMark turns the shading off — but the deletion strikethrough stays, because a row on its way out has to be visible.

  • Add a row, edit another and delete a third to get all three colours.
  • Press Change colours on / off — the shading goes.
  • Check that the deleted row keeps its strikethrough.
  • The + * - markers in _st stay as well.
