14. Other

14-1. Undo and redo (Ctrl+Z · Ctrl+Y)

Every change is recorded as you make it. Ctrl+Z steps back one at a time and Ctrl+Y steps forward again, and each step says which action it is undoing.

  • Mix a few cell edits, row insertions and deletions.
  • Press Ctrl+Z and read the messages.
  • Press Ctrl+Y to put them back.
