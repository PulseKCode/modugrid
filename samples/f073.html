8.1 Image transfer

8.1-5. upload — imageUpload is required

Choose upload and forget imageUpload and nothing is uploaded at all. A warning is written to the browser console, so that is the first thing to check when photos refuse to go.

  • The left grid has imageUpload; the right one is missing it on purpose.
  • Add a photo to each Photos cell.
  • The right one does not progress, and a warning appears in the console (F12).
with imageUpload — works
without it — nothing happens
