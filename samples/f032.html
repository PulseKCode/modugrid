4. Selection

4-9. Reading the selection — arrays of ids

getSelected() and getChecked() hand back arrays of ids. That is what you want to post to a server; when you need the row contents, look them up in getFilteredData().

  • Select a few rows and tick a few others.
  • Press Show the ids to see what comes back.
  • Look up the rows shows the usual way to turn ids into row objects.
