12.2 Fonts

12.2-2. Changing the font size

Enlarge the body font and the row numbers and numeric cells grow with it. They are sized in proportion rather than fixed, so the table stays balanced at any size — handy for a zoom control or a per-user preference.

  • Try 12px, 15px and 18px in turn.
  • Watch the # column and the Score and Salary cells scale as well.
  • Passing a plain number to setFont() changes the body only.
