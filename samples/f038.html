5.1 Entry points

5.1-6. Delete clears cells — a whole range at once

Delete empties the current cell, or the entire selected range if you have one. It counts as a single step, so one Ctrl+Z brings everything back.

  • Drag out a range of several cells.
  • Press Delete — the whole range is emptied.
  • Press Ctrl+Z once and check that all of it returns.
