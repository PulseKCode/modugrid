5.1 Entry points

5.1-5. Enter moves down · Esc cancels

Enter commits and moves down, Shift+Enter moves up. Esc throws away what you typed and puts the original value back.

  • Edit the Score cell and press Enter — you drop down a row.
  • Start editing another cell and press Esc — the old value returns.
  • It is the quickest way to fill a single column from top to bottom.
