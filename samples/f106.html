14. Other

14-2. Row ids are filled in for you

Rows arriving without an id, or sharing one, are sorted out as they load. A number once used is never handed out again, so deleting a row and adding another cannot leave you with two of the same. Supply genId to number them your own way.

  • Load messy data feeds in rows with ids missing and duplicated.
  • Look at how they were sorted out.
  • Press Add, delete, add and check that the number is not reused.
  • Use order numbers switches to an ORD-0001 style.
