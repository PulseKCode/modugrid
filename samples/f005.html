2.3 System columns

2.3-2. Recolouring the row gutter (--bg-gut)

The system columns use a different background in the body, the way Excel shades its row gutter. If the shade does not suit your screen, change the single --bg-gut variable.

  • Notice the far-left cells are shaded differently from the data cells.
  • Press Like normal cells and the distinction disappears.
  • Press Accent shade to use a colour of your own.
