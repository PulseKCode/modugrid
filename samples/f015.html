3.2 Filtering

3.2-2. Select all in the filter popup

The Select all box at the top of the popup has three states — everything ticked, some ticked (shown as an indeterminate mark) and nothing ticked. The selected / total count sits beside it.

  • Open the Role funnel — everything starts ticked.
  • Untick a single entry and the box switches to the in-between state.
  • Click select-all to clear everything, then click again to tick everything.
