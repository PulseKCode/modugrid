4-8. Extending a range with Shift and the arrow keys

In cell mode this grows a rectangular block the way a spreadsheet does; in row mode it grows a run of rows. A range you started with the mouse can be extended from the keyboard.

  • Click a cell, then hold Shift and press ↓ and →.
  • Pressing an arrow on its own drops the range and moves one cell.
  • Press Switch to row mode and try Shift+↓ again.
