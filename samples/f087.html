10. Layout

10-5. Building group headers (two rows)

Gather related columns under a shared caption and the header becomes two rows. Give the columns the same group name and that is all. On a wide table it tells you at a glance what kind of value each column holds.

  • The header is split into Personal and Performance.
  • # and Name have no group, so they span both rows.
  • Resize a column and the group caption follows.
  • Scroll down and the two-row header stays in place.
