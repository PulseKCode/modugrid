10. Layout

10-4. Freezing columns

Pin the first few columns so they do not scroll away sideways. On a wide table it keeps you from losing track of which row you are reading. Mark the columns with freeze:1 and switch freezeOn on.

  • Scroll the grid sideways — # and Name stay where they are.
  • Press Freezing on / off and everything scrolls together.
  • Add freeze:1 to more columns to pin more of them.
