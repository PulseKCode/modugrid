3.2 Filtering

3.2-7. Leaving one column out (noFilter)

Adding noFilter:1 to a column definition removes just that funnel. It suits free-text columns such as a note, where every value is different and a list would be useless.

  • The Name and Note headers have no funnel.
  • Role and Score still do.
  • A column without a funnel can still be found through the search box.
