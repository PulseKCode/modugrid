12.1 Themes

12.1-1. Switching themes (eleven of them)

One call to setTheme() recolours the whole grid. Themes are applied per grid, so two tables on one page can look quite different, and nothing else on the page is touched.

  • Use the two pickers above to set each grid separately.
  • The two grids show different colours at the same time.
  • To adjust one colour only, pass variables as well — setTheme('ocean', {ac:'#ff6600'}).
