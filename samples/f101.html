12.3 Display options

12.3-2. Messages appear inside the grid

Notices such as "copied" or "nothing to undo" appear in the middle of the grid concerned rather than in a corner of the page. With several grids about, there is no doubt which one is talking.

  • Press Message on the left — it appears over the left table.
  • Press the right-hand button and it appears there instead.
  • Selecting a range and pressing Ctrl+C gives a notice in that grid too.
