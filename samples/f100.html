12.3 Display options

12.3-1. Choosing what appears in the footer

The footer carries the row count, the page list and Per page, in that order. Any of them can be switched off, and turning all three off removes the footer altogether.

  • Switch Row count, Page list and Per page off one at a time.
  • Whole footer removes it in one go.
  • On a read-only screen you might keep the page list and nothing else.
