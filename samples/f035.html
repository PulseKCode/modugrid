5.1 Entry points

5.1-3. Clicking another cell saves what you typed

If you wander off to another cell in the middle of an edit, what you had typed is committed first and the editor follows you across. Nothing is lost for want of pressing Enter.

  • Start editing the Name cell and change the text.
  • Instead of pressing Enter, click a different cell.
  • The earlier value is saved and the editor moves to the new cell.
