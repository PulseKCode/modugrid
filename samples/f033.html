5.1 Entry points

5.1-1. Three ways to start editing a cell

With editMode on, editing begins on a double-click, on F2, or simply by typing. All three open the same editor, so the cell never shifts under your hands.

  • Double-click the Name cell.
  • Press Esc, then F2 on the same cell.
  • Press Esc again, leave the cell selected and just start typing.
