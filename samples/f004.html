2.3 System columns

2.3-1. Row number, status and checkbox columns (_rn · _st · _cb)

_rn (row number), _st (change status) and _cb (checkbox) only need to appear in cols. Wherever you declare them they end up on the far left and stay put when you scroll sideways.

  • _cb is deliberately declared last, yet it shows up first on screen.
  • Scroll the grid sideways — the three left-hand columns stay where they are.
  • Use the buttons to turn each of them on and off.
