5.3 Input control

5.3-4. Reordering rows and changing row heights

Drag the # cell up or down to move a row. Drag its lower edge and that one row grows taller, which is handy when a note runs long.

  • Drag the # cell up or down to reorder.
  • Drag the bottom edge of a # cell downwards to make that row taller.
  • Reordering is disabled while a sort is applied, since the sort decides the order.
