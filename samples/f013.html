3.1 Sorting

3.1-5. Removing sorts from the sort bar

A sort bar appears above the grid as soon as anything is sorted. Drop one condition with the ✕ on its chip, or clear the lot with Clear.

  • Press Role ↑ then Score ↓ to build two conditions.
  • Click the ✕ on a chip to remove that one.
  • Press Clear to remove them all.
