3.1 Sorting

3.1-4. Sorting on several columns

Pass multiSort:true and sorts stack up. Each column gets a 1, 2, 3 order badge in the order you clicked, and each one cycles through ascending, descending and off on its own.

  • Click the Role sort icon — badge 1.
  • Then click Score — badge 2. Rows group by role and sort by score inside each group.
  • Click Role twice more to switch it off; the score sort stays.
