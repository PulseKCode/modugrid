10. Layout

10-2. Double-click a boundary to fit the contents

Double-click the edge of a header and the column is sized to its contents, exactly as a spreadsheet does. It measures what is on the screen rather than the raw value, so money with separators and coded names are not clipped.

  • Double-click the right edge of the Note header.
  • Try it on Salary (separators) and Status (a coded name) too.
  • The buttons do the same thing.
