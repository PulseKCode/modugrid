14-5. Trimming the right-click menu (contextMenu)

Entries you do not want can be dropped from the right-click menu. Anything you do not mention stays, and the setting sits on top of the permissions — turn something on here and it still will not appear without the right to use it.

  • With the default, right-click a row to see the full menu.
  • Switch to without copy and delete and right-click again.
  • The entry names are detail · edit · copy · copyExcel · insertAbove · insertBelow · tree · delete.
