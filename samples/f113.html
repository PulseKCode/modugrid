14-9. Wiring up your own buttons (controls)

Point options.controls at buttons and labels of your own and the grid keeps their pressed state and their text up to date as things change. Use it when you would rather build your own toolbar than use the grid's.

  • The buttons in the grey area belong to this page, not the grid.
  • Press them to switch the grid features on and off.
  • Watch their pressed state and the labels beside them follow along.
