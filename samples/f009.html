3.1 Sorting

3.1-1. Sorting is done with the ▲▼ icons

Click the ▲▼ icons at the right of a header to sort. The header label is reserved for moving the column, so dragging the text reorders the columns instead.

  • Click the text of the Score header — nothing is sorted.
  • Click the ▲▼ icon on the same header — now it sorts.
  • Drag the header text sideways to move the column.
