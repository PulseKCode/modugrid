9-2. Pasting a block — one undo puts it back

A block copied out of a spreadsheet pastes in with Ctrl+V. However many cells it fills, a single Ctrl+Z undoes the whole thing.

  • Click the box below to select it all and copy.
  • Click the first Name cell and press Ctrl+V.
  • Press Ctrl+Z once and check that everything comes back.
