5.1 Entry points

5.1-4. Tab and Shift+Tab — moving sideways

Tab commits the value and moves right, Shift+Tab moves left. At the end of a row it carries on to the next one, which makes filling a row at a time straightforward.

  • Edit the Name cell and press Tab.
  • Keep pressing Tab to the end of the row — it continues on the next row.
  • Walk back with Shift+Tab.
