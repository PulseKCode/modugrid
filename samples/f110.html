14. Other

14-6. The menu setting cannot outrank a permission

Entries you do not mention stay visible — the setting lists what to hide rather than what to show. And because it sits on top of the permissions, switching something on here will not bring it back if the user has no right to it.

  • Only delete named, and allowed — only {delete:true} is set, yet the other entries are still there.
  • Named but not allowed — {delete:true} is set but canDelete:false, so it does not appear.
  • Allowed and named — now it does.
