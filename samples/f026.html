4. Selection

4-3. The header follows the rows immediately

Every time you tick or untick a row the header mark is brought up to date. Tick the last outstanding row and the header fills in; untick any one and it drops back to the in-between state.

  • Tick the rows one by one — the header fills in as you tick the last one.
  • Untick any single row and the header goes back to in-between.
  • Clear them all and the header clears too.
