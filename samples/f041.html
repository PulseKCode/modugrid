5.2 Edit permissions

5.2-2. Locking individual rows and cells

Lock a whole row when its status is closed, or lock one cell when the figure belongs to somebody else. Use both together and the row lock is applied first.

  • Rows whose Status is Inactive are locked outright — double-clicking does nothing.
  • The Salary cell only opens on rows scoring 80 or more.
  • A locked cell gives no hover highlight, so it reads as locked before you try it.
