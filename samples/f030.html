4. Selection

4-7. Select all from your own toolbar — toggleCheckAll()

Call toggleCheckAll(true/false) from a button of your own. The result is the same as clicking the header, and so is the scope: the rows currently on screen.

  • Press Tick all and Clear all.
  • Move to another page and press it again — only that page is affected.
  • Apply a filter and press it; only the surviving rows are ticked.
