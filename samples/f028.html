4. Selection

4-5. Putting a word in the checkbox header (cbHeader)

Replace the select-all checkbox with a label such as Select. It suits screens where an accidental select-all would be expensive.

  • The left grid is the default, the middle one says Select, the right one is blank.
  • Clicking the header does nothing on the middle and right grids.
  • The row checkboxes still work in all three.
default — a checkbox
cbHeader:'Select'
cbHeader:'none'
