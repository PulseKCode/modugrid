1-2. Several grids on one page — themes and fonts too

Grids never interfere with each other. Sorting and selection are separate, and so are themes and fonts, which makes it easy to place two very different tables side by side.

  • Switch the left grid to the ocean theme with a larger font — the right one stays as it is.
  • Sort the left grid with its sort icons; the order on the right does not move.
  • Press Compare both to see the two independent states.
