5.3 Input control

5.3-3. Adding, inserting and deleting rows

addRow() appends at the end; insertAt() puts a row above or below the selected one. The Insert Above and Insert Below entries in the right-click menu call exactly the same thing.

  • Select a row and press Insert above or Insert below.
  • Right-click a row for the same menu.
  • A new row is marked with + in the _st column.
