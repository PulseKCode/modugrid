8.1 Image transfer

8.1-1. Choosing how photos reach the server (imageMode)

This setting decides when a photo is uploaded and in what shape it travels. Preview only? none. Large files? upload. One save for everything? multipart. Something small like a signature? base64.

  • Use the table below to pick the mode that matches your case.
  • Each mode is covered one at a time in the pages that follow.
  • A word of warning — do not change the mode after the screen is up. Photos already held are stored differently per mode, so the transfer would not line up.
