3.4 Grouping and tree

3.4-2. Showing a hierarchy as a tree

Point each row at its parent with parentId and mark rows that have children with _hc:true. Use it for org charts, category trees, bills of materials — anything with a parent and child relationship.

  • Press Tree view — only the top level is left.
  • Click ▸ to open a branch.
  • Right-clicking a row lets you expand and collapse as well.
