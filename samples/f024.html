4. Selection

4-1. Clicking a row and ticking it are two different things

Rows you click and rows you tick are kept as separate lists. That lets you treat one as "what I am looking at" and the other as "what I am about to act on" — browse by clicking, tick the few you actually need.

  • Click two or three rows to select them; the checkboxes do not move.
  • Tick the checkbox on a different row; the selection does not move.
  • Press Show both lists to see that they differ.
