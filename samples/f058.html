7. Validation

7-4. How long the error tooltip stays

An invalid value raises a ⚠ message tooltip that fades after a while — three seconds by default, adjustable with errorMsgDuration. Pass zero or less and it stays until the value is fixed.

  • Type 150 into Score to raise the tooltip — it goes after three seconds.
  • Switch to 1 second and try again.
  • Switch to never fade and the message stays put.
